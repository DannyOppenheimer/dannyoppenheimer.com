Idea 1:

When my profile picture is clicked on, enter "dance party" mode. Each blade of grass (including jumbo ones) 
is turned into a random color from colors that seem dance or rave themed (bright, fun, neon, etc...). My name will 
fade between fun neon colors as well.

During this time, mouse influence will be turned off completely, and 
the blades will perform a "dance". They will do the following moves in order
- Extend northwest, return to center
- Extend northeast, return to center
- Extend southwest, return to center
- Extend south east, return to center
- Alternating for each adjacent blade, extend and spin a 360 clockwise or counter clockwise, then return to center
(i.e. for a subset of the grid, a 2x2: the top left and bottom right will spin counter clockwise, top right and bottom left will spin clockwise)
- Grass blades will extend in multiple directions at once (not usually possible during regular mouse movement, but will be during dance party)
-- turn into a "+" shape, looking like a star, return to center
-- turn into a "x" shape, looking like a star, return to center
- For the last part of the dance, blades will pulse by changing size. They will either be small, or big (1.5x their size)
-- First, pulse back and forth 3 times of being small and big, but adjacent dots will be on different sizes for one step
(i.e. for a subset of the grid, a 2x2: the top left and bottom right will be big, top right and bottom left will be small, then they all switch)
-- Second, they will act like water and show ripple animations of a wave of big dots propagating over the field.
--- These ripples will first go from bottom left to top right
--- These ripples will next make cool symmetrical shapes eminating from the centers of the screen, kind of like what sand look likes when you put it on a speaker.
- Return to normal
